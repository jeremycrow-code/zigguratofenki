23 April 1951 – 30 November 2016. Yonkers history teacher. Patriarch of the Old Templar Church. The man who dubbed the 13 October 2000 class.

Offices that are well attested

  • Sovereign Grand Master, Ancient and Primitive Rite of Memphis-Misraïm for the United States, listed from 1998. His widow Mary Lou Cappello is listed from 2010 on the US rite’s own succession table.
  • Past Grand Master, Martinist Order of the Temple (family obituary).
  • Member, Huguenot Lodge No. 46 F.&A.M.; Royal Arch; Cryptic; Grand College of Rites; Royal Order of Scotland. The obituary also names Bethlehem Crusader Knights Templar and a “Knights Templar Order of the Temple.” Those last two names are the family’s wording, not a corporate ID we will tighten for them.
  • Signatory milieu of the 1991 North American Gnostic concordat and participant in the Synod of 2000. Episcopal record: Tau Mikaël I dossier.

29 December 2001 (other-order chronicle)

The published historical chronology of SMOTH MIT states, under the year 2001:

(29-12) Se funda el Gran Priorato de Estados Unidos, a cargo de Ronald Cappello. También se crea el Priorato de la Florida, a cargo de Mons. Orlando Hipólito Lima.

Source: granmaestrazgointernacionaltemplario.org/sobre-nosotros.

That sentence is a later Spanish administration’s timeline. It is not a scan of a 2001 charter in this archive. We include it because it names Cappello in the same international Templar field that used OSMTH-family names, and because it sits after a night in which he told a Yonkers class they were taking American and Portuguese lines. It does not prove that the 2000 chapter was an act of the 2001 priorate. The priorate, if the chronicle is right, did not yet exist.
