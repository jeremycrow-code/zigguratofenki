Context

The ziggurat is a working image: a stepped structure raised so that repair can be done, not so that the plain can be abandoned.

Knights of the Ziggurat are not a military chapter of OSMTH. Inside the Novus Ordo Luciferi they sit after the alchemical and infernal work, as an Order of Chivalry that treats the planetary spheres as things to be healed rather than fled. The school curriculum lives at novusordoluciferi.org. The episcopal registry lives at gnosticluciferian.org.

This page is a map. It is not the inner papers.

This site records specific acts and papers named on the Claims page. School membership in the Novus Ordo Luciferi is not knighthood in the Knights of the Ziggurat.
