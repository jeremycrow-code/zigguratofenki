The 2000 Investiture

Act of Friday, 13 October 2000, Yonkers, New York.
Documented Act

On Friday, 13 October 2000, Jeremy Crow received the solemn accolade of knighthood in the Sovereign Military Order of the Temple of Jerusalem (OSMTH, American and Portuguese successions). The investiture was performed during a private vigil chapter convened in Yonkers, New York.

Conferring Prelate

The dubbing was administered by The Most Rev. +Ronald V. Cappello (Tau Mikaël I), Patriarch of the Old Templar Church and high-ranking officer of the modern Neo-Templar succession. His ministerial dossier as a bishop within the Independent Sacramental Movement is indexed at gnosticluciferian.org.

The Assembly and Date

The chapter admitted a class of twelve candidates alongside Crow. The date was selected for its traditional resonance within Templar history, marking the anniversary of the arrests ordered by Philip IV on Friday, 13 October 1307.

Transmission of Faculties

During this investiture, Patriarch Cappello formally conveyed full faculties to dub and create new knights under the lineage held by his office. Jeremy Crow explicitly declines to use this faculty to create members of OSMTH or SMOTJ. Instead, that tactile sword-transmission serves as the chivalric lineage brought forward into the new, independent foundation of the Knights of the Ziggurat.

Archive Custody

Primary instruments, certificates, and physical registers of this act are held in custody in the archives of the Canadian See.
