Documented act

Friday, 13 October 2000. Yonkers, New York.

Jeremy Crow received the accolade of knighthood in the Sovereign Military Order of the Temple of Jerusalem. The chapter was a private vigil in the residence of Patriarch Ronald V. Cappello (Tau Mikaël I).

The room

Twelve candidates were knighted, Crow among them. Cappello, as conferring prelate, made thirteen people in that temporary chapter. The date was the anniversary of the 1307 arrests.

What Cappello said that night

He stated that the candidates were being knighted in both the American and Portuguese successions. Crow’s recollection is that Cappello said he had just returned from Portugal. That remark is memory, not a boarding pass. It is recorded here as such.

Faculties

Cappello conveyed faculty to dub new knights under the lineage of that office. Crow does not use that faculty to create members of OSMTH, SMOTJ, OSMTHU, or any other existing Templar administration. The tactile line is carried into a new Order with its own charter.

Papers

Instruments of this act are held in the archives of the Canadian See of Cerinthus. Cappello’s episcopal dossier is at gnosticluciferian.org.
