Frequently Asked Questions

Archival clarifications and definitions.

1. What is ZigguratOfEnki.org?

ZigguratOfEnki.org is the official chivalric registry for the Knights of the Ziggurat. It provides archival documentation for a knighthood conferred on 13 October 2000 and a foundational charter dated 4 November 2026. It serves as a public ledger and boundary marker.

2. What is the Knights of the Ziggurat?

The Knights of the Ziggurat is an independent Order of Chivalry functioning as an Inner Order of the Novus Ordo Luciferi. It treats knighthood as an operative spiritual discipline focused on macrocosmic balance and planetary remediation rather than social prestige. Entry into the Order is optional and distinct from school membership.

3. Did Jeremy Crow become a Knight Templar in 2000?

Jeremy Crow was dubbed a Knight in the Sovereign Military Order of the Temple of Jerusalem (OSMTH) on 13 October 2000 by Patriarch Ronald V. Cappello in Yonkers, New York. That investiture included explicit faculties to dub new knights. Crow does not use that authority to create OSMTH members.

4. Is this the OSMTH?

No. This site is not OSMTH and does not represent any grand priory of that organization. It records a historical accolade received within that lineage without claiming current administrative standing or jurisdiction inside it.

5. Is this Palaprat’s Order of the Temple?

No. The Knights of the Ziggurat is a new foundation. It references the 4 November 1804 election of Bernard-Raymond Fabré-Palaprat solely as a historical and commemorative anchor for its 222-year charter.

6. Does joining the Novus Ordo Luciferi make someone a Knight?

No. Membership in the Novus Ordo Luciferi school does not confer knighthood, titles, or chivalric status. The Knights of the Ziggurat is a separate path that must be earned independently of the general school curriculum.

7. What does the 4 November 2026 date mean?

The date marks the official signing of the Charter of the Knights of the Ziggurat. It falls precisely 222 years after the 4 November 1804 Paris Convent that initiated the modern Neo-Templar revival. The date is a symbolic marker; the legal act is modern and independent.

8. What is not claimed on this site?

This site does not claim British state honors, membership in the Order of the Thistle, active seats in OSMTH chapters, or that the Larmenius Charter is a 1324 medieval document. It makes no claims of continuous corporate descent from the medieval Poor Fellow-Soldiers of Christ.

This site records specific acts and papers named on the Claims page. Profiles of other orders are historical notes. They are not membership, faculty, or endorsement. School membership in the Novus Ordo Luciferi is not knighthood in the Knights of the Ziggurat.
