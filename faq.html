Frequently Asked Questions

Home Claims FAQ

What is ZigguratOfEnki.org?

A chivalric registry for the Knights of the Ziggurat. It records a knighthood conferred on 13 October 2000 and a charter dated 4 November 2026. It is not the school and not the episcopal archive.

What is the Knights of the Ziggurat?

A new Order of Chivalry. It functions as an Inner Order of the Novus Ordo Luciferi. Entry is optional and is not the same as school membership.

Did Jeremy Crow become a Knight Templar in 2000?

He was dubbed a Knight in the Sovereign Military Order of the Temple of Jerusalem on 13 October 2000 by Ronald V. Cappello in Yonkers. Cappello named American and Portuguese successions. Twelve candidates were knighted. Faculty to dub was conveyed. Crow does not use it to make OSMTH members.

Is this the OSMTH?

No. This site does not represent OSMTH, SMOTJ, OSMTHU, or SMOTH MIT. It records one historical accolade.

Was Cappello Grand Prior of the United States?

A later Spanish Templar chronology (SMOTH MIT) says a Grand Priory of the United States was founded on 29 December 2001 with Cappello in charge. That notice is their chronicle, not a document in this archive. It postdates the Yonkers night by thirteen months.

Is this Palaprat’s Order of the Temple?

No. 4 November 1804 is a commemorative anchor for the 2026 date. The Order is new.

Does joining the Novus Ordo Luciferi make someone a Knight?

No. The school does not confer knighthood.

What is not claimed?

No British state honours. No Order of the Thistle. No current OSMTH seat. No 1324 Larmenius instrument. No corporate descent from the medieval Temple.

This site records specific acts and papers named on the Claims page. School membership in the Novus Ordo Luciferi is not knighthood in the Knights of the Ziggurat.
