A new Order of Chivalry. A recorded 2000 dubbing. A charter dated 4 November 2026.

The Knights of the Ziggurat (KotZ) is a new Order. It inherits a sword-line from a knighthood conferred on Jeremy Crow on Friday, 13 October 2000, by Patriarch Ronald V. Cappello in the Sovereign Military Order of the Temple of Jerusalem. Cappello named both the American and Portuguese successions that night. This site does not admit members into OSMTH. It does not claim to be Palaprat’s Temple. School membership in the Novus Ordo Luciferi does not make anyone a Knight.

  • 29 December 2001 — A later Spanish Templar chronicle lists Cappello as head of a newly founded Grand Priory of the United States. That notice is not the source of the 2000 faculty. See Cappello.
