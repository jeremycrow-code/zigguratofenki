Knights of the Ziggurat

A new Order of Chivalry. A recorded 2000 dubbing. A charter dated 4 November 2026.

The Knights of the Ziggurat (KotZ) is a new Order. It inherits a sword-line from a knighthood conferred on Jeremy Crow on Friday, 13 October 2000, by Patriarch Ronald V. Cappello in the Sovereign Military Order of the Temple of Jerusalem. It does not admit members into OSMTH. It does not claim to be Palaprat’s Temple. School membership in the Novus Ordo Luciferi does not make anyone a Knight.

Record Timeline

  • 13 October 2000 Dubbing of Jeremy Crow into OSMTH, private vigil, Yonkers, NY.
  • 25 September 2026 Episcopal certificate of Tau Cerinthus III (fons honorum for the chivalric charter).
  • 4 November 2026 Solemn issuance of the Charter of the Knights of the Ziggurat.
  • 21 December 2026 Gnostic Temple of the Morning Star charter (separate see, documented at gnosticluciferian.org).
