Context

Several living bodies use OSMTH, OSMTJ, OSMTHU, SMOTJ, or SMOTH in their names. They do not share one administration. This page is a map of that field. It is not a claim to any of them.

  • Swiss NGO OSMTH and the American SMOTJ are one cluster, often described as the UN-recognized line. This site does not represent them.
  • OSMTJ / Fontes is another Portuguese-rooted cluster.
  • OSMTHU formed around the 1999 election of Fernando de Toro-Garland after the IFA split from the Fontes regency. Portuguese memorial pages later name Toro-Garland and Ronald Cappello in the same breath. That is association, not a shared corporate seal.
  • SMOTH MIT is a later Spanish-led body whose public chronology lists a United States Grand Priory founded 29 December 2001 under Cappello. See Cappello.

The 13 October 2000 chapter used the name Sovereign Military Order of the Temple of Jerusalem and, by Cappello’s spoken formula that night, American and Portuguese successions. KotZ does not continue any of the administrations above. The analogy, if one is needed, is the Military Order of Christ: new charter, inherited tactus, different name.

This site records specific acts and papers named on the Claims page. Profiles of other orders are historical notes. They are not membership, faculty, or endorsement.
