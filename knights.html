Regulations Governing the Roll

  • Being enrolled as a student in the Novus Ordo Luciferi school does not confer knighthood.
  • Being invested as a Knight does not confer ordination into the Gnostic clergy.
  • Any knight who falsely claims official affiliation with OSMTH, foreign chivalric orders, or historical state priories based upon this accolade will be formally expunged from this register.
This site records specific acts and papers named on the Claims page. Profiles of other orders are historical notes. They are not membership, faculty, or endorsement. School membership in the Novus Ordo Luciferi is not knighthood in the Knights of the Ziggurat.
