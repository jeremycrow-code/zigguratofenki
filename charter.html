Foundational Charter

Instrument of Foundation: 4 November 2026.
Documented Act

Notice of Promulgation: The charter of the Knights of the Ziggurat will be dated 4 November 2026. That date is commemorative: it marks exactly 222 years since Bernard-Raymond Fabré-Palaprat was elected Grand Master at the General Convent of Paris on 4 November 1804. This Order is not Palaprat’s Order. The legal and theurgic act is issued from an independent See.

Charter Parameters

Upon formal execution on 4 November 2026, the complete instrument will be published on this register with the following provisions:

  • Issuing Authority: The Most Rev. +Jeremy Crow (Tau Cerinthus III), exercising canonical fons honorum as an autocephalous Gnostic Bishop.
  • Title of the Body: The Knights of the Ziggurat.
  • Jurisdiction: An autonomous chivalric order operating as an Inner Order of the Novus Ordo Luciferi. It is an independent charter, not a warrant into OSMTH or any existing European body.
  • Candidate Selection: Knighthood is not automatically conferred upon members of the Novus Ordo Luciferi school. Candidates must complete specific microcosmic cultivation and demonstrate personal independence prior to investiture.
  • Standing and Disavowal: While the tactile accolade of the blade is historically indelible, formal standing within the Order and inclusion on its public register are conditional. The Chancery reserves the absolute right to strike individuals from the Roll, revoke any delegated faculties, and formally disavow anyone who makes false claims of affiliation with OSMTH, historical state orders, or foreign bodies based upon this accolade.
