Sovereign Instrument

Instrument dated 4 November 2026. This date is commemorative, marking exactly 222 years after Bernard-Raymond Fabré-Palaprat's sitting of 4 November 1804. This Order is issued from an independent See, carrying forward the living sword-line into a new sovereign vehicle.

Terms of the Charter

  • Issuer: The Most Rev. +Jeremy Crow (Tau Cerinthus III), holding the fons honorum as bishop of the Gnostic Temple of the Morning Star.
  • Name: The Knights of the Ziggurat.
  • Standing: An autonomous Order of Chivalry functioning as the third Inner Order of the Novus Ordo Luciferi. It operates independently of OSMTH or any European administrative body.
  • Entry: School membership in the Novus Ordo Luciferi does not automatically confer knighthood. Candidates are evaluated and selected separately based on merit and inner development.
  • The Roll: Standing on the public register requires adherence to the principles of the Order. False claims of state order or external administrative affiliations from this accolade are grounds for removal from the roll.

Status of Publication: The signed official text and registry documents are maintained within the archives of the Canadian See of Cerinthus and published on the public roll as inductions take place.
