Statement of Claims and Non-Claims

Jurisdictional boundary of the Knights of the Ziggurat.
Documented Act

1. Documented Facts

  • 13 October 2000: Jeremy Crow was dubbed Knight in the Sovereign Military Order of the Temple of Jerusalem (OSMTH, American and Portuguese successions) by +Ronald V. Cappello (Tau Mikaël I) in a private vigil at Yonkers, New York, among a class of twelve candidates. Faculty to dub in that Order was conveyed during this chapter. He does not use this faculty to make OSMTH knights.
  • 25 September 2026: Episcopal certificate issued for Tau Cerinthus III, Gnostic Temple of the Morning Star. This autocephalous Gnostic episcopate supplies the fons honorum used to charter a new chivalric order. It is not a royal patent, not an apostolic delegate credential from Rome, and not an OSMTH warrant.
  • 4 November 2026: The formal Charter of the Knights of the Ziggurat is executed under that episcopal authority. It establishes a new rule, a new name, and a new Order.

2. What Is Not Claimed

  • No membership in OSMTH after 2000, and no current administrative office or authority exercised inside OSMTH or SMOTJ.
  • No identity with Bernard-Raymond Fabré-Palaprat’s 1804 Ordre du Temple, or with any living organization using that name.
  • No membership in the Order of the Thistle, and no royal or state patent from Great Britain.
  • No claim that the Larmenius Charter is a valid 1324 medieval instrument.
  • No claim that membership in the Novus Ordo Luciferi school constitutes chivalric investiture.
  • No claim that this registry endorses, governs, or represents any other historic or contemporary order referenced in its documentation.

3. Open Questions (Unproven Hypotheses)

  • Whether the physical Larmenius manuscript was penned prior to 1804 as an 18th-century noble ledger, or fabricated entirely during the Napoleonic period.
  • Whether a chivalric current originating from the Order of the Thistle passed through Jacobite military lodges into the Chevalier Kadosh, Cossé-Brissac, and the 1804 Paris chapter.

Tactus Versus Institutional Membership

In classical esoteric transmission, an accolade of the sword confers tactus (physical contact with an operative current). It does not convey corporate stock, voting rights, or a perpetual seat in the chapter of the conferring prelate. The Knights of the Ziggurat operates on inherited tactus and independent episcopal fons honorum. It does not look back to petition old administrations for recognition.

This site records specific acts and papers named on the Claims page. Profiles of other orders are historical notes. They are not membership, faculty, or endorsement. School membership in the Novus Ordo Luciferi is not knighthood in the Knights of the Ziggurat.
