Documented act / Reconstruction / Open question

Jurisdictional boundary of the Knights of the Ziggurat.

1. Documented facts

  • 13 October 2000: Jeremy Crow was dubbed Knight in the Sovereign Military Order of the Temple of Jerusalem. Cappello stated that the accolade was in both the American and Portuguese successions. Private vigil, Yonkers. Twelve candidates were knighted. Cappello made thirteen people in that temporary chapter. Faculty to dub in that Order was conveyed. Crow does not use that faculty to make OSMTH knights.
  • 25 September 2026: Episcopal certificate issued for Tau Cerinthus III, Gnostic Temple of the Morning Star. That See supplies the fons honorum used to charter a new Order. It is not a royal patent and not an OSMTH warrant.
  • 4 November 2026: Charter of the Knights of the Ziggurat under that episcopal authority. New rule. New name. New Order.

2. Other-order chronicle (not our paper)

  • 29 December 2001: The public chronology of SMOTH MIT (Gran Maestrazgo Internacional Templario) states that a Grand Priory of the United States was founded that day “a cargo de Ronald Cappello,” with a Priory of Florida under Orlando Hipólito Lima. That is their published timeline, written long after the event. We have not published a scan of that 2001 instrument. It cannot be the legal source of a dubbing that happened thirteen months earlier. It is listed here because it is consistent with Cappello already working that international field in 2000, including his remark that he had just returned from Portugal.

3. What is not claimed

  • No membership in OSMTH after 2000, and no office now exercised inside OSMTH, SMOTJ, OSMTHU, or SMOTH MIT.
  • No identity with Palaprat’s 1804 Ordre du Temple, or with any living body using that name.
  • No membership in the Order of the Thistle, and no British state patent.
  • No claim that the Larmenius Charter is a valid 1324 instrument.
  • No claim that school membership in the Novus Ordo Luciferi is knighthood.
  • No endorsement of any other order named in the notes.

4. Open questions

  • Whether the physical Larmenius manuscript was written before 1804.
  • Whether a Thistle current passed, as sword-tactus, through the Jacobite / Kadosh / Cossé-Brissac story into Palaprat.

Tactus versus membership

An accolade confers contact with a current. It does not confer a seat in the conferring chapter. The Knights of the Ziggurat works from the 2000 tactus and a new 2026 charter. It does not petition old administrations for recognition.

This site records specific acts and papers named on this page. Profiles of other orders are historical notes. They are not membership, faculty, or endorsement. School membership in the Novus Ordo Luciferi is not knighthood in the Knights of the Ziggurat.
